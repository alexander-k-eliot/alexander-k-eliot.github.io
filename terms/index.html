2. What the Services are

The free AI-visibility checker

You submit a URL; our system fetches publicly available pages at that address and reports what it found — things like whether an llms.txt exists, whether structured data is present, and whether an AI crawler is blocked. It produces a score. That score is our opinion, computed by our own published methodology, not an industry standard or a measurement of anything an AI vendor actually does. It is free, rate-limited, and offered as-is.

Pulse

A paid subscription that re-runs those checks against your site on a schedule and emails you a report, so you can see change over time. Tiers, cadence, and site counts are described on the Pulse page and are what you get. Pulse tells you what our checks found on the dates they ran. It is not a real-time alerting system and does not promise to catch every change.

Digital products

Playbooks, kits, templates, and similar one-time downloads, sold through our storefront provider. You get the files described on the listing.

The Checkable Standard and free materials

An open specification and supporting materials, published for anyone to use. See section 8.
