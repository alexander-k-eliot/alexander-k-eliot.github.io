What this actually shows

These are real, established local businesses across two unrelated Georgia metros. Insurance brokers, security companies, a senior-care franchise, a design-build contractor, a national telecom, a fire-protection contractor, a car dealership. None of them asked to be checked. Athens's scorable llms.txt rate (33%) is meaningfully higher than metro Atlanta's (11%) on this small sample. Not something we'd claim as a real regional trend off 9 and 9 sites each, but a genuine, real difference worth naming plainly rather than averaging away. Both numbers are within the range of every other vertical we've benchmarked this year. Chamber-of-commerce membership isn't a startup-only or big-company-only gap, it's just the default state of the web.

Methodology

Every result above comes from a real, direct server-side fetch of the business's own homepage and root-level `llms.txt`/`robots.txt`, dated 2026-07-26, using a clearly-identifying User-Agent. llms.txt was only counted as real if it returned a genuine `text/plain` response, not an HTML soft-404 (an earlier pass on this exact data caught 3 false positives this way, corrected before publishing. Worth stating plainly rather than hiding). AI-crawler blocking checked against the same 8 user-agents used across every benchmark on this site. This is a lighter, 4-point spot-check, not the full 9-point weighted score used on the main benchmark pages. Stated honestly, not implied to be more than it is.
