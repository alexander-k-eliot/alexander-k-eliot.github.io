The State of AI Visibility 2026. Hospitality Edition

As trip planning shifts to AI agents that book, compare, and recommend on a traveler's behalf, a hotel brand's homepage has a second audience beyond browsers: it has to let assistants in the door at all. We ran our honest 9-point AgentReady audit against 14 major hotel brand homepages to see who's reachable and who's actually readable.

56.3/100 average score among the 4 sites that let our fetcher in. Median 50. That "4" is not a typo. The real story this edition is how few brands could even be measured.

10 of 14 major hotel brands blocked the audit outright

This is the highest block rate we've seen in any edition of this benchmark. Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, Four Seasons, Best Western, Radisson, Omni, and Kimpton all returned HTTP 403 to a single, clearly-identified, respectful request. Choice Hotels' homepage timed out entirely. Only Wyndham, Accor, Ritz-Carlton, and Fairmont let the audit run. Among that small scorable group, budget-positioned Wyndham posted the best score in the entire field at 90/100. Beating Ritz-Carlton, one of the most prestigious names in luxury hospitality, which scored just 45/100, and Fairmont, another storied luxury brand, which came in lowest at 35/100. The pattern worth naming: the hotel industry's bot-defense posture is aggressive enough that most flagship brands were invisible to our clearly-identified fetcher, and likely to any crawler they haven't specifically allowlisted, whether or not their actual content is any good. Re-measured 2026-07-30 on the corrected engine: the same ten brands still refuse the fetcher. Wyndham still leads the scorable four (87/100). One real mover: Fairmont re-measured at 72/100, up from 35, so its earlier last place no longer stands.

Methodology

Six server-side requests per site (the public homepage plus robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, agents.md), clear identifying User-Agent, 9 machine-readability checks (llms.txt, llms-full.txt, agents.md, AI-crawler access in robots.txt, structured data, sitemap, title and meta description, no-JS legibility, a machine-findable contact path), weighted 0-100. Homepage-only snapshot, not a full-site audit, dated 2026-07-22. Public data only, no personal data, one request per site. Scores change as sites change.
