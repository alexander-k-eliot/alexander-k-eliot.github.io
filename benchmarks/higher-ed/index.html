The State of AI Visibility 2026. Higher Education Edition

College search is now AI-assisted before it's ever human. Students and parents ask ChatGPT and Gemini to compare programs, costs, and deadlines before they open a single admissions page. If a university's homepage isn't machine-readable, the assistant answering that question can't see it, no matter how prestigious the name on the gate.

58.8/100 average score among the 12 sites that let our fetcher in, as measured 2026-07-22. Median 62.5. Not one university in this sample broke 70/100 at that time.

MIT scored lowest of any school we could measure (as originally measured)

Of the 12 universities our fetcher could reach on 2026-07-22, MIT came in dead last at 35/100. Behind every large state school in the sample, including Arizona State and UT Austin. The gaps were structural, not cosmetic: no XML sitemap, no meta description on the homepage, and no schema.org markup of any kind, on the website of the institution most identified with technology itself. At the other end, four schools tied for the top score in this benchmark, Boston University, Carnegie Mellon, Harvard, and UC Berkeley, all at 70/100, which is still only the mid tier, not the top "excellent" tier used elsewhere on this site. No university homepage we could reach that round earned it.

Re-measured 2026-07-31 on the corrected engine (full 231-site re-measure, raw data public in benchmark-data): MIT is no longer lowest -- it rose to 40/100. NYU is now the lowest scorer at 10/100, down sharply from its original 65/100. Harvard and UC Berkeley now clear 70, at 72/100 each, so the "not one university broke 70" finding no longer holds. The numbers above are the original 2026-07-22 measurement, kept for the record.

Methodology

Six server-side requests per site (the public homepage plus robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, agents.md), clear identifying User-Agent, 9 machine-readability checks (llms.txt, llms-full.txt, agents.md, AI-crawler access in robots.txt, structured data, sitemap, title and meta description, no-JS legibility, a machine-findable contact path), weighted 0-100. Homepage-only snapshot, not a full-site audit, dated 2026-07-22. Public data only, no personal data, one request per site. Scores change as sites change.
