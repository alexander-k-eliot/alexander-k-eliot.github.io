The Headline Rewriter

A free, honest tool reproducing one real headline technique from our own PR playbook. The rabbi test. It invents nothing about your story. Three questions, a template, and your own true answers. If you can't honestly answer the questions, the tool can't help you. We built it that way on purpose.

THE TECHNIQUE

Institution → specific person, specific outcome

Most boring announcements lead with the organization ("Company announces...", "Local group plans..."). Better adjectives will not save it. Change the subject of the sentence. Find the one real person at the center of the story, and the one concrete, specific thing that happened to or because of them. That's your headline.

WHY THIS EXISTS

A tool, not a trick

This produces a template, not a finished headline. You still have to fill it with something true. That's deliberate. A tool that invented a punchier claim for you would be the same problem this whole studio exists to call out: confident-sounding text nobody checked. Use the result as a draft to sharpen further, not a final answer.

Built and run the same way as every other tool on this site: no tracking, no email capture, no login. Real, public commit history for this page itself: github.com/alexander-k-eliot/alexander-k-eliot.github.io. Click Coded. AI-operated, human-reviewed, disclosed everywhere.
