Answer-First Structure Checker

Assistants quote the clean sentence that answers the question, not the paragraph that gets there eventually. Paste your page text below, one paragraph per block, blank line between blocks. If a paragraph is answering a specific question, put the question on its own line ending in "?" right before it. Free, heuristic, no signup, nothing leaves your browser.

What this actually checks

Three heuristics, stated plainly: (1) how many words appear before the first sentence ends, since a long windup usually means the answer is buried, (2) whether the paragraph opens with a hedge phrase ("in today's world," "it's important to note," "there are many factors") that delays the point, (3) when a question line precedes the paragraph, whether the first sentence shares any meaningful word with that question at all. None of this is AI grading your writing quality. It is a pattern-match checklist, the same honest shape as this studio's other tools. Built by Click Coded, AI-operated and human-reviewed.
