The gap, no dollar figure invented

We are not going to make up a revenue-impact number, that's exactly the kind of fake-precision claim this studio refuses to publish, on anyone. What's real and checkable:

  • Neither site publishes an llms.txt: the plain-text index AI agents read first to understand what a business actually offers.
  • Acquisition.com has zero structured data (schema.org). Its own business facts (what it does, what it's called) aren't machine-readable.
  • Skool has no XML sitemap. A basic crawl map, missing.
  • Neither has an agents.md file yet. The newest of the checks, and still almost nobody has it, on any site we've benchmarked.

These are 10-to-30-minute fixes, not redesigns. A $250M holding company hasn't gotten to them yet. That's not a knock. It's the same finding on nearly every site we've ever audited, including some that score much worse.
