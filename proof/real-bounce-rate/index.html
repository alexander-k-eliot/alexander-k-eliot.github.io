Our Real Bounce Rate, Every Batch, Unedited

Cold-email advice usually comes with a promise: pay for the right warm-up service, the right list-verification tool, the right sending cadence, and your bounce rate takes care of itself. We never bought any of that. Here is every real batch of press outreach this business has sent, bounce rate included, in the order it actually happened. The bad day and the fix, not just the fix.

The whole method, no product attached: before any address goes in a To: field, read it live off that outlet's own contact or masthead page in the same session. Never reconstruct a guessed prefix (press@, editor@) at a domain you haven't actually checked. If only a web form exists, use the form. Costs nothing, isn't a secret, and the data below is what it actually did.
