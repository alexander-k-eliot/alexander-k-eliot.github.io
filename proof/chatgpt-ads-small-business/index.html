ChatGPT shows ads now. Here's what that means if you run a small business.

The search world's oldest split, organic versus paid, just arrived inside AI assistants, a year earlier than most forecasts. An honest explainer. We don't sell ads management, so read this as reporting, not a pitch.

As of early July 2026, ads appear in 51% of US free-tier ChatGPT replies. On July 22, OpenAI opened a self-serve Ads Manager. Any business can now buy placement targeted on conversational context. OpenAI projects $2.5B in ad revenue this year and $11B next year. Whatever you think of it, the auction era of AI answers has started.

The three things worth understanding

01

Paid placement doesn't fix unreadable

An ad can put your name in a reply. It can't make the assistant able to read your site when a real customer asks a real question about you, and 93% of Google AI Mode sessions already end without a click, which means the answer itself is the battlefield. Organic legibility got more valuable the day ads launched, not less, because it is the only presence that shows up in the unpaid 49%.

02

The split will professionalize fast

Search history is repeating: first organic, then ads, then an industry of ad managers. Expect "ChatGPT ads agencies" within months. Some will be good. The bad ones will sell impressions inside answers nobody asked for.

03

What a small business should do this quarter costs nothing

Make sure the assistants can read you at all. Crawler access, structured data, extractable content, reviews. That's the free layer under any future ad spend, and most sites fail it today (average score across 158 real homepages we audited: 65/100).

Start with the free layer: check your site's AI readability. 30 seconds, no signup, shows its work. We'll say this plainly: we do not manage ad spend, and nobody should promise you AI rankings, paid or organic.
