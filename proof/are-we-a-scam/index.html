What this actually means

If someone asks either of these assistants right now whether to trust this specific business, they get either no answer, or a real, favorable-sounding verdict that belongs to someone else entirely. Neither outcome is something we can fix by trying harder. It's a structural gap in how these systems disambiguate two similarly-named, unrelated companies. We've already taken real technical steps on our side (a documented Organization entity, disambiguating language, a clarified sameAs graph). This page is the honest, current status of whether that's actually working from the outside, not a claim that it is.
