Methodology and honest scope

Checks performed 2026-07-25 against each company's live public homepage: robots.txt (present/absent, AI-crawler directives), llms.txt (present/absent, verified as real plain-text content and not a soft-404 HTML page served under a 200 status), XML sitemap reachability, homepage <title>, meta description, canonical tag, JSON-LD structured-data blocks, and H1 heading presence. The same categories this studio's free public checker runs, performed manually here since browser-based cross-site reads are blocked by CORS for external domains (a real, disclosed limitation of the free tool, not a workaround). Scores are directional (~) rounded estimates from the same weighting as this studio's published 158-site industry benchmark, not a decimal-precision claim. La Equidad, a football club Reynolds and Rob McElhenney had a reported stake in, was excluded after confirming the club was dissolved in December 2025. It no longer exists to audit. Minority/passive investments without an operating website under Reynolds' control (1Password, Wealthsimple, Nuvei) were excluded as out of scope for a "does your site talk to AI" question.
