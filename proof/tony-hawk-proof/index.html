We Checked Whether AI Assistants Can Read Tony Hawk's Sites

Not a full "portfolio," because Tony Hawk doesn't really have one in the Reynolds/MrBeast sense, and we're not going to pad a report to pretend otherwise. Just three real sites: his own tonyhawk.com, Birdhouse Skateboards (the brand he actually founded and still skates for), and The Skatepark Project (his nonprofit). Same free, disclosed, falsifiable check every time: can ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and the rest actually read these pages, or are they citing someone else?

16 H1 headings on one page of tonyhawk.com. A page is supposed to have one. A crawler trying to figure out what the page is actually about has sixteen competing answers.

The surprising find

Birdhouse Skateboards. Birdhouseskateboards.com

A real robots.txt most sites twice its size don't have

This one genuinely surprised us. Birdhouse's robots.txt doesn't just list crawler rules. It publishes a real agents.md and UCP/MCP endpoints for AI shopping agents, with an explicit rule requiring human approval before any purchase completes. That's the same agent-commerce infrastructure we found on MrBeast's Lunchly earlier today, on a skateboard brand founded in 1992. Someone on that team is genuinely ahead of the curve. The gap is just that the actual homepage content (JSON-LD, headings) hasn't caught up to the crawler policy yet.

Methodology and honest scope

Checks performed 2026-07-25 against each site's live public homepage: robots.txt, llms.txt (both verified as real plain-text files and not soft-404 HTML pages served under a 200 status), XML sitemap reachability, homepage <title>, meta description, canonical tag, JSON-LD structured-data blocks, and H1 heading count. birdhouseskateboards.com returned blocked/empty responses to a direct server-side fetch (likely bot-detection on that specific request path). Re-verified via a real rendered browser session using same-origin fetch calls before reporting, rather than assumed broken or skipped. Scores are directional (~) rounded estimates using the same weighting as this studio's published 158-site industry benchmark. Only three sites are included because only three real, distinct Tony Hawk-affiliated websites were found. This page will not be padded with tangential licensing/game pages to inflate its scope.
