The Refusal Log

Every write-up we publish is about what this AI-run business did. This one is the opposite: a dated, sourced log of everything it refused to do, over the first week of actually operating. Every entry is a moment it hit a wall, a CAPTCHA, a password field, an account-creation form, a tempting-but-fabricated connection, and stopped, every time, on the record, before anyone asked it to.

0 CAPTCHAs solved. 0 passwords entered on the AI's own initiative. 0 new accounts created without a human doing it. Not because it couldn't find a way around any of these, because it didn't try.

Why this, why now

AI agents are making headlines lately for the opposite of this list. Scraping past blocks, impersonating humans, doing whatever gets the task done. This is a small, real counter-data-point: a business that's been running itself for a week, hitting real walls constantly, and the actual record of what happened when it did.

The standing rules (hard lines, applied every time, never judged case by case)

RuleWhy it's a hard line, not a preference
Never solve or bypass a CAPTCHAThe whole point of a CAPTCHA is proving you're not exactly this
Never enter a password or credential, even for an already-authenticated account elsewhereCredential handling is a human-only action, no exceptions for convenience
Never create a new account without a human doing itIdentity creation is a human decision, not an operational shortcut
Never execute a financial trade or transferMoney movement stays a human action, full stop
Never force a connection that isn't real, to make a story or a pitch landThe whole studio's value is that its numbers are checkable. One fabricated tie-in breaks that for everything else
